📨 Sending + reply alerts (optional)

A small free script in your Google account sends applications from your Gmail with your resume attached, then pings your phone when a company replies. Only you can use it. This part is easiest on a computer, and takes about 10 minutes, once.

1 Resume in Google Drive

Upload your resume PDF to Google Drive, open it, and copy the ID from the link: drive.google.com/file/d/this-part/view

2 Get phone alerts with ntfy

ntfy (say "notify") is a free app that pops up a notification on your phone, like a text. No account or sign-up needed. You do this part on your phone.

  1. Copy your private channel name. It's made just for you, so only you get your alerts:
  2. Install ntfy. 📲 iPhone: App Store🤖 Android: Google Play
  3. Open ntfy and tap Allow when it asks to send notifications. (Missed it? iPhone Settings → Notifications → ntfy → Allow Notifications.)
  4. Tap the + button, paste your channel name into the Topic box, and tap Subscribe. Leave the server setting alone.
  5. Test it:
    Your phone should buzz within a few seconds.

Nothing came? Check the name in ntfy matches exactly (no extra spaces) and that notifications are allowed. Alerts also always go to your email, so ntfy is a bonus.
